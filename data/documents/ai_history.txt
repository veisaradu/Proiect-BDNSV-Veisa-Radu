History of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has a rich history spanning several decades. The field was officially founded in 1956 at the Dartmouth Conference, where the term "artificial intelligence" was coined.

Early Years (1950s-1960s):
- Alan Turing proposed the Turing Test in 1950
- First AI programs developed for theorem proving and game playing
- High optimism about AI's potential

AI Winter (1970s-1980s):
- Limitations of early AI systems became apparent
- Reduced funding and interest in AI research
- Focus shifted to expert systems

Revival (1990s-2000s):
- Machine learning gained prominence
- Deep Blue defeated chess champion Garry Kasparov
- Increased computational power enabled new approaches

Modern Era (2010s-present):
- Deep learning revolutionized AI
- Breakthroughs in computer vision, natural language processing
- AI assistants, autonomous vehicles, and more
- Large language models like GPT emerged

Today, AI is transforming industries and society, with applications in healthcare, finance, transportation, and countless other domains.
